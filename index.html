A little help

Clear choices, clear answers.

Questions about Whirlpick or a test build? Write to poonamgaggarapps@gmail.com.

Does Whirlpick need an account?

No account or developer backend is part of the app. Core picking and grouping work on your device. Optional purchases use Apple or Google, and sharing opens the service you choose.

What do weights mean?

A choice with weight 2 has twice the chance of a choice with weight 1. Excluded choices have no chance in that round. Groups use equal random shuffling, regardless of weights.

When can I download it?

Native Android and iOS builds are being developed and independently tested. Store links will appear after device acceptance and store release.
