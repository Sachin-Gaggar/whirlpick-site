Privacy, plainly.

Development draft · 27 September 2026

This draft describes the intended first version of Whirlpick by Poonam Gaggar Apps. The app is in development. We will verify and update this policy against the release build before distribution.

What the app keeps on your device

Whirlpick stores the lists and labels you enter, choice weights, used entries in no-repeat rounds, recent selection/group results and your preferences. Results retain snapshots so later edits do not change earlier outcomes. The app keeps its most recent 200 results and a previous-good local recovery snapshot.

No account, developer-operated cloud service, advertising SDK or analytics SDK is part of this version. Core selection and grouping do not send your lists to us. The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location or notification access.

Backups and sharing are your choice

JSON backups, CSV exports and shared result text can contain your labels and group members. They are readable files, not an encrypted vault. When you choose a file location, mail app or sharing service, that provider handles the exported information under its own policy.

Operating-system device backups or transfers may include app data depending on the platform and your settings. Those services are separate from a Whirlpick cloud account. See Apple's iCloud backup explanation and your Android device's backup settings.

Optional store purchases

Planned one-time Pro purchases and restoration use Apple or Google. The app verifies store transactions and keeps local entitlement status. The store handles payment and account information; we do not receive your payment-card details. Purchase ownership is not included in a list backup, and no cross-store purchase entitlement is promised.

Editing and deleting data

You can edit or delete saved lists and clear result history. A recovery snapshot can retain earlier records until it is replaced. Delete all app data is intended to remove current and recovery files on that installation. Previously exported files, email, operating-system backups and copies saved by other services must be managed separately.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the email and attachments you choose to send. Please include only the information needed to investigate your question. This website has no custom analytics, advertising or externally hosted fonts. An appearance preference is saved in your browser when available.

The public site is designed for GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting links or using email opens a separate provider.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when relevant app behavior changes. For privacy questions or support-email deletion requests, contact poonamgaggarapps@gmail.com.
